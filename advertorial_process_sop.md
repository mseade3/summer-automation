# Advertorial Process SOP

Use this SOP from Cursor Chat or Composer by referencing `@advertorial_process_sop.md`.

## Cursor Execution Prompt

```text
Act as a direct-response copy chief. Follow @advertorial_process_sop.md exactly. Build me a conversion-focused advertorial for this offer:
[PASTE OFFER DETAILS HERE]
```

## Objective

Create advertorials that pre-sell skeptical traffic, deepen problem awareness, and transition naturally into a product CTA.

## Inputs Required

- Product and category
- Ideal customer profile
- Core pain and desired outcome
- Offer details (price, bundles, guarantees, shipping)
- Proof assets (reviews, UGC, before/after, expert references)
- Compliance boundaries (medical claims, banned language, policy limits)

## Workflow

### Step 1: Offer and Audience Clarity

- Define one primary reader persona.
- Define one dominant pain, one emotional consequence, and one desired transformation.
- Clarify mechanism: why this product solves what alternatives fail to solve.

### Step 2: Angle Shortlist

- Generate 5-7 possible angles.
- Score each by novelty, believability, emotional intensity, and evidence strength.
- Choose top 2 angles and state why.

### Step 3: Hook and Headline Drafting

- Write 10 headline options across styles:
  - Contrarian
  - Curiosity
  - Problem agitation
  - Mechanism reveal
  - Proof-led
- Pick best 3 and explain channel fit (Meta native, blog-style, long-form page).

### Step 4: Narrative Skeleton

Build structure in this order:

1. Pattern interrupt hook
2. Problem amplification with customer language
3. Failed alternatives
4. New mechanism introduction
5. Proof sequence
6. Product bridge
7. Offer stack
8. Risk reversal
9. CTA close

### Step 5: Draft Generation

Produce:

- Full long-form advertorial draft
- 3 CTA block variants
- 3 lead paragraph variants
- 5 subhead options

### Step 6: Credibility and Compliance Pass

- Replace vague claims with specific proof.
- Add qualifiers where required.
- Flag any unsupported superlatives, guarantee language, or prohibited claim areas.

### Step 7: Conversion Optimization Pass

- Tighten readability and remove fluff.
- Increase scannability using short paragraphs and strategic subheads.
- Ensure every section advances belief or buying intent.

## Required Output Format

For each run, return:

1. Chosen angle and rationale
2. Final headline options (top 3)
3. Full advertorial draft
4. CTA variants
5. Evidence checklist (what proof was used where)
6. Risk notes (weak points likely to suppress conversion)
7. A/B test plan (hook, proof placement, CTA framing)

## Non-Negotiables

- Do not invent customer proof or statistics.
- Do not use hype language without evidence.
- Keep voice specific, concrete, and emotionally grounded.
- If evidence is weak, flag it clearly before finalizing draft.

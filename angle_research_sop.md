# Angle Research SOP

Use this SOP from Cursor Chat or Composer by referencing `@angle_research_sop.md`.

## Cursor Execution Prompt

```text
Act as a growth strategist for paid social and landers. Follow @angle_research_sop.md exactly. Research winning ad angles for this offer:
[PASTE PRODUCT + AUDIENCE BRIEF]
```

## Objective

Identify and prioritize ad/message angles most likely to win for cold traffic before creative production.

## Inputs Required

- Product and category
- Core audience and sub-segments
- Current offer and price point
- Existing ad/creative performance data (if any)
- Competitor examples and known claims

## Research Workflow

### Step 1: Problem and Desire Map

- List top 5 pain points in customer language.
- List top 5 desired outcomes in customer language.
- Pair each pain with its highest-value emotional consequence.

### Step 2: Voice of Customer Mining

Pull phrases from:
- Product reviews
- Reddit threads
- Amazon reviews
- YouTube comments
- Niche forums

Capture verbatim lines with source links.

### Step 3: Competitor Messaging Scan

- Analyze 10-20 ads or landing pages in the category.
- Extract repeated claims, hooks, promises, and differentiators.
- Mark saturation level: low, medium, high.

### Step 4: Angle Generation

Generate 12-20 candidate angles across buckets:
- Problem-solution
- Mechanism
- Identity/status
- Fear/loss avoidance
- Aspiration/transformation
- Speed/convenience
- Cost/value

### Step 5: Angle Scoring

Score each angle 1-10 on:
- Emotional pull
- Believability
- Proof availability
- Novelty
- Policy/compliance risk

Then compute overall weighted score and rank.

### Step 6: Creative Translation

For top 5 angles, generate:
- One primary hook
- One visual concept
- One CTA framing
- One objection-handling line

## Required Output Format

For each run, output:

1. Top 5 angles ranked
2. Scorecard table (with rationale)
3. Voice-of-customer quotes used
4. Hook examples per top angle
5. Creative brief starter lines
6. Testing plan:
   - 3 angles to test first
   - Budget split suggestion
   - Win/loss criteria

## Non-Negotiables

- Do not use angles without evidence from customer language or proof.
- Do not rely only on internal assumptions.
- Do not recommend claims that are legally or platform risky.
- Flag weak proof areas before creative production.

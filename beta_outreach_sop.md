# Beta-Tester Outreach SOP

Use this SOP from Cursor Chat or Composer by referencing `@beta_outreach_sop.md`.

## Objective

Recruit 5–10 real, local beta testers for the Passive Leg Exerciser in order to
collect **genuine, legally usable testimonials** that can fill the
`[INSERT REAL TESTIMONIAL]` placeholders in the advertorial — with zero fabricated
proof and full FTC compliance.

## Non-Negotiables (read before sending anything)

- **No fabricated endorsements.** Only quote people who actually used the product.
- **Disclose the material connection.** Every published testimonial from a tester who
  received a free unit MUST carry a clear disclosure, e.g. *"Participant received a free
  testing unit."* (FTC Endorsement Guides + 2024 Rule on fake/deceptive reviews.)
- **Get a written testimonial release.** Honest feedback does not grant the right to use
  someone's words/name/likeness in ads. Get explicit written permission to publish.
- **Recording consent (LEGAL).** Maryland is an **all-party-consent** state for recording
  calls. If any participant or the caller is in MD, you must get spoken consent on the
  recording before capturing audio. When in doubt, ask first every time.
- **No medical claims, even verbally.** Ask how their legs *feel* (comfort, heaviness,
  stiffness). Do not ask about, or imply, circulation, swelling, clots, or any condition.
- **Duty of care.** Include a safety note in outreach. Confirm the device is electrically
  safe (intact plug/cord, no damage) before shipping. Tell testers to stop if they feel
  pain and to check with their doctor if they have any leg/circulation condition.

## Outreach Script (email / community board / organizer)

```text
Subject: Local student project: 5-10 retired adults wanted to try a free seated mobility device

Hi [Name/Group Organizer],

My name is Miles Seade, and I'm a student entrepreneur working on a health and
mobility project this summer.

I'm developing a compact, mechanical Passive Leg Exerciser for older adults who spend a
lot of the day seated (recliner, desk, or watching TV) and notice that "heavy, stiff,
afternoon leg" feeling. Before any wider launch, I want honest feedback on whether it
genuinely feels comfortable and helpful to use.

I'm looking for 5 to 10 local participants for a small, informal testing group. Here's
the deal, in plain terms:

- I cover everything. I'll ship you a brand-new unit, free, to keep.
- In return, I'd ask you to use it while seated most days for about 2 weeks, then share
  honest feedback (a quick 5-minute phone call or a short written note is perfect).
- Your feedback may be used. With your written permission, I may quote your comments in
  marketing, noted clearly as "received a free testing unit." You can decline this and
  still keep the device.
- A quick safety note: this is a comfort/mobility device, not a medical treatment. Please
  check with your doctor first if you have any leg, circulation, or mobility condition,
  and stop using it if anything feels uncomfortable.

If you'd like to take part (or know someone who would), reply here or text me at
[YOUR NUMBER]. Happy to answer any questions.

Thank you for your time!

Best regards,
Miles Kofi Seade
Student Entrepreneur
```

## Execution Steps

1. **Source channels:** family/personal network, local senior centers, faith groups,
   Nextdoor (Clinton, MD / Ann Arbor, MI). Personal intros convert best.
2. **Screen lightly:** confirm they sit a lot, have no condition that makes passive leg
   movement risky, and are willing to give feedback. Capture mailing address + contact.
3. **Confirm device safety, then ship:** inspect the unit (cord/plug intact). Note that
   overseas drop-ship can take 1–3 weeks — plan ~6 weeks total (ship → 2-week use →
   feedback). For speed/quality control, consider buying a small batch yourself and
   shipping from home rather than blind drop-shipping to a senior's door.
4. **Collect feedback with consent:** if recording, get spoken consent first ("Is it okay
   if I record this so I quote you accurately?"). Otherwise take written notes.
5. **Get the release:** a one-line written/email OK: *"You have my permission to use my
   feedback, first name, and city in your marketing."* Keep it on file.
6. **Harvest verbatim phrases:** capture their exact comfort/feel language (e.g., how their
   legs feel by late afternoon).
7. **Plug in with disclosure:** paste real quotes into the `[INSERT REAL TESTIMONIAL]`
   slots, each tagged *"Participant received a free testing unit."*
